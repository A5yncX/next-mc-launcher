# Create 0.9.0 behavior acceptance

Target: the 106 Create blocks in the existing Horizon 9f42064 client export, with Pumpkin authoritative. The pinned Horizon port is the executable reference; Java Create 6.0.10 is the supplementary reference when the port's expected behavior is unclear. This is not a port of blocks absent from this registry.

Every block below must pass the applicable behavior cases in a disposable world, with two clients for observable effects. Verify placement, break/drop rules, invalid interactions and persistence across a clean server restart. Entries without item forms are tested through the gameplay feature that creates them, not by giving them an artificial item. A registry row, rendered model or animation alone does not count as completed behavior.

**Status: 0/106 fully accepted.** All 106 are registered. See the per-block ledger below for what is working; only `Complete` means the acceptance cases passed and no known pinned-port behavior is missing.

## Verified batches (not a full group sign-off)

- [Registry, first placement cases, and motor](README.md): 106 blocks registered; six placement cases and `32 → +64 → −96 RPM` motor control tested.
- Drivetrain: large/small −2 ratio, flywheel, encased ports and gearbox on two clients, plus reload (`drivetrain-smoke-final.log`). All six bare-to-encased survival interactions retain the casing. Both clients saw the cog power a shaft at 32 RPM; closing the east port stopped it. Rotation and port settings persisted, sneaking with the wrench stripped the casing, and mining returned only the gear (`encasing-live.log`, `SMOKE_EXIT=0`). Overload and wider network cases remain.
- Controls: clutch and gearshift redstone detach/reverse, reconnect and reload on two clients (`controls-smoke.log`). Gauges report 32/0 RPM and correct capacity on both clients, feed vanilla comparator/dust, and reload; simulated load and overload in Rust (`gauge-smoke.log`, `gauge-tests.log`). Hand crank +32/−32 RPM via real clicks, expires, remains idle on restart, and drops once on support loss (`crank-smoke.log`). The chain drive and adjustable gearshift link neighboring parts, accept a real wrench click, and transmit 32/16/50/64 RPM at analog inputs 0/15/8/15 on the corresponding input or output side; break/reconnect and restart verified (`chain-smoke-v2.log`). A real loaded network now reads 256 stress at 32 RPM with the mechanical press; live overload and mixed consumers remain.
- Water: small wheel material and `+8 → 0 → −8 RPM`, reload (`water-smoke-v3.log`). Large wheel obstructed placement, eight parts, `+4 RPM`, reload and single drop on part removal (`large-water-smoke-v2.log`). Chunk-boundary and alternate orientation cases remain untested.
- Materials: the pinned pack loads 22 vanilla-station recipes (4 crafting, 18 stonecutting) and loot tables for 24 passive materials and supported machine fixtures. Live mining checked the zinc iron-tier gate, raw zinc, Silk Touch on both ore blocks, casing drops and reload (`passive-smoke-v2.log`). The recipe loader test checks both namespaced items and tags (`passive-tests-v2.log`). Andesite/brass casing application to drivetrain parts is verified above. Six pinned stripped-wood casing recipes now pass real survival use, including item cost, rejection for the wrong ingredient/unstripped wood/sneaking, and restart (`material-smoke-v2.log`, `SMOKE_EXIT=0`). The expanded two-client mining run verified all 24 ordinary drops, four tier gates, three axe drops, Fortune III and Silk Touch on both ores, and Silk Touch after reload (`passive-enchanted-smoke-v2.log`, `SMOKE_EXIT=0`). Initial Fortune failures came from selecting and enchanting without waiting for the server's held-slot update. Same-seed fresh Overworld comparison and dual-client reconnect after a clean save confirmed both natural zinc variants (`passive-retry-final.log`, `ZINC_EXIT=0`); the baseline had no Create zinc. The live recipe sweep verified all four crafting and 18 stonecutting transactions, exact authoritative consumption/outputs and inventory after restart (`actuator-recipes-accept.log`, `RECIPES_EXIT=0`). Both sides use the pinned recipe file order.
- Depot: hand-insert, swap, empty-hand retrieval after restart and dropping the stored stack on removal (`depot-smoke-v3.log`); two unit tests cover HeldItem and OutputBuffer save/load (`passive-tests-v2.log`). Automated transfer remains.

- Mechanical press: Depot cycle, clear-gap requirement, real stress budget, and two consumed iron ingots yielding two recoverable sheets after reload (`press-final.log`, `basin-press-regression.log`). No input consumed when the Depot output buffer is full (`press-tests.log`). Both clients also saw an unpowered loose stack stay intact, then one strike split two iron ingots into one ingot and one sheet; the exact stacks and completed strike persisted across restart (`press-world-smoke.log`). Both clients saw the horizontal belt hold its two ingots at the press. Power was removed before the strike; after restart and restoring power, exactly one sheet and one ingot remained (`press-belt-smoke.log`, `SMOKE_EXIT=0`). The unit test also reloads a press after its strike to verify it cannot claim the same strike again (`press-belt-unit-v3.log`). Basin compacting is covered below. Fluid recipes and full recipe integration remain.

- Item drain: manual buckets and water bottles, empty-container exchange, no mixed fluid or overflow, comparator outputs 10/12/15 and persisted 1500 mB read by real clients (`drain-final.log`, `drain-tests.log`). Automatic cargo and pipe extraction remain.

- Sails: 16 color states and sail frame behavior through the shared adapter; live click verification of red repaint with facing preserved, coplanar spread, shears, frame barrier, assisted extension and exactly one white sail dropped after reload (`sails-final-v2.log`). Windmill assembly remains.

- Fluid tank: single-block storage rejects mixing and preserves water/lava across restart (`tank-v2.log`). Both clients verified 1×1×2 and 2×2×1 assembly, 9000 mB through the upper part, child comparator, wrench-controlled window, and lava light. Removing the restored controller leaves 8000 mB recoverable from the surviving upper part (`tank-multi-final-v2.log`). Same-chunk only; bottles and larger edge cases remain.

- Mechanical pump: neighboring cog drives the pump at −32 RPM, direct tank-to-tank transfer, wrench reversal, stopped flow after power removal, exact 1000 mB conserved and recoverable after restart (`pump-smoke-final.log`). Rust tests cover overflow, no cross-fluid transfer and conservation (`pump-units.log`).

- Fluid pipes and valves: survival placement creates a tee on both clients; opening with positive RPM splits each 1000 mB input into 500/500 mB, closing with negative RPM or removing a pipe stops flow, reattaching restores it, and restart preserves 1000 mB at each outlet with the valve closed (`pipes-final-v2.log`). Single-tank tee regression also passes with controller-aware transfer (`tank-multi-pipes-regression.log`). Two clients verified forward and reverse pumping through upper ports on two multiblock tanks without losing any of the 9000 mB, followed by restart and bucket recovery (`tank-upper-ports-v8.log`, `SMOKE_EXIT=0`). Open ends, pressure and animation remain.

- Millstone and fan: both live clients see the fan at 32 RPM, range `5.125 → 1 → 5.125` with a wall, and stop on loss of power. The millstone uses the same kinetic graph; 2 andesite became 2 cobblestone and both outputs were recovered after restart (`factory-acceptance.log`, `FACTORY_EXIT=0`). Unit tests cover the pinned recipe set and the output-capacity guard.

- Chutes: both live clients see 16 stone held at a blocked midpoint while four remain at source. Restoring the target transfers all 20; smart-chute redstone stops and resumes exactly the expected items, and both destination inventories survive restart (`chute-save-final.log`, `SMOKE_EXIT=0`).

- Mechanical drill: two clients received crack stages and their clear packet at 32 RPM. One stone became one cobblestone drop; bedrock and chest were left intact. After restart, the drill resumed only when power was restored (`drill-smoke-v2.log`, `SMOKE_EXIT=0`).
- Stationary mechanical saw: two clients saw 32 RPM and all three unattached logs removed with exactly three drops. Supported trees and stone were untouched (`saw-smoke.log`). The upward saw cut one andesite alloy into six shafts at +32 RPM, held all six behind an obstruction through restart, then ejected exactly six (`saw-processing-smoke-v2.log`, `SMOKE_EXIT=0`).
- Sticker: two clients verified south-facing placement into existing redstone power, rising-edge toggle and preserved state after restart (`sticker-smoke-v2.log`, `SMOKE_EXIT=0`). Moving adhesion is not implemented.
- Chassis: two clients verified placement, all three default range tags, glue application, repeat application and empty-hand sneak clearing. Restart preserved the remaining glue and all three ranges (`chassis-smoke.log`, `SMOKE_EXIT=0`). Unit test covers accepted range endpoints, malformed settings and persisted non-default range (`chassis-build.log`).
- Fan item processing: both clients saw four authoritative media segments, two inputs per lane, then two iron ingots, two infested stone, two cooked beef and two packed ice after restarting mid-cycle (`fan-medium-smoke-v2.log`, `SMOKE_EXIT=0`; `fan-medium-units.log`, three passed). Fixtures contain the water source so loose items remain in the wash stream.

- Burners: two clients verified filled and empty survival placement, cage ignition and soul-flame conversion, coal/cake fuel tiers, comparator outputs 2/3, goggles and creative cycling. Fuel and goggles persisted after restart (`burner-smoke.log`, `SMOKE_EXIT=0`; `burner-unit.log`, one passed).
- Basin processing: both clients saw the mixer and press at 32 RPM. Hoppers fed all four pinned item-only recipes: nine snow blocks produced one ice, one copper and one zinc ingot produced two brass only after a real coal click heated the burner, and both nugget variants produced one andesite alloy each. All outputs persisted (`basin-smoke.log`, `SMOKE_EXIT=0`; `basin-units-v2.log`, five passed including no input loss when output is blocked).

- Funnels: both clients verified survival placement, manual insertion and vertical transfer, basic brass sample filtering, powered blocking and restart (`funnel-smoke-v6.log`, `SMOKE_EXIT=0`). Currently limited to single chests and barrels; advanced filters and long-hold count settings need live acceptance.
- Belt: both clients saw real four-part formation and 32 RPM on the belt and separate output shaft. An obstructed path cost nothing, formation cost one connector, and breaking the reloaded belt returned one connector and restored both end shafts (`belt-locked-smoke.log`, `SMOKE_EXIT=0`). Both clients also saw a seven-item loose quartz stack move to a blocked end, persist after restart, and eject with no count loss after unblocking (`belt-cargo-smoke.log`, `SMOKE_EXIT=0`). Slopes and chunk boundaries are still separate checks.
- Gantry: both clients verified real carriage placement, rejection without item loss on an invalid mount, three-rail shape and shared power, and −32 RPM forwarded by the stationary carriage. Breaking the rail stopped the output; restoring it and restarting recovered it (`gantry-smoke-v3.log`, `SMOKE_EXIT=0`). Assembly movement is not implemented.
